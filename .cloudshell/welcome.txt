   ConsoleVault  ·  deploy into your own Google Cloud project
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   ▶  To install, type this and press Enter:

          ./bootstrap.sh

   It signs you in, lets you pick your project and region from a
   menu, and deploys everything (~10 minutes). No Terraform, no
   image build, no OAuth setup — it prints a URL + password at the
   end that you use to sign in.

   Make sure billing is enabled on your project first.
   Docs: README.md   ·   Questions: https://dimo.com.au
